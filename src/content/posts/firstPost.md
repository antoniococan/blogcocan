This first post is an introduction of what's currently going on in my life, how I set up my [academic homepage](https://antoniococan.github.io/) and my [blog](https://antoniococan.github.io/blogcocan/); and mainly about how to blog with AstroPaper so that I have a place to fall back to.

## Current life

I finished my PhD thesis and sent it out for examination this November. The decision from the pre-examiners is supposed to be made by the end of this month. After that -- and depending on the decision -- I will be allowed to defend my thesis. This will happen most likely in January and I will officially graduate in January or February. I can't believe that it's actually happening! I feel nervous and excited but also a tad melancholic. I will have more to write about my PhD journey in [this blog post](https://antoniococan.github.io/blogcocan/posts/phdjourney/).

I am preparing for my defense. At the same time I am making preparations for my karonkka and looking for postdoctoral opportunities. Let's see where my journey takes me!

## Setting up my homepage

I have coded my own websites before in HTML and CSS (and a bit of JavaScript). But I'm mildly irritated about having to make sure that websites work for different screen sizes, different browsers, etc. So this time around I decided to take a template and modify it to my hearts content. I went with [Sen Li's template](https://github.com/senli1073/academic-homepage-template/) and used the Harvard Crimson color. I've never coded a blogging system and since I don't have much time (nor interest!) to invest in doing so, I decided to incorporate a blogging system. For this I went with [AstroPaper](https://astro.build/themes/details/astropaper/) due to its minimalistic design.
