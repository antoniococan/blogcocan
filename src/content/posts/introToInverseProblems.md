## Introduction

Most of the mathematics you have seen thus far runs forwards. Given initial conditions, solve the differential equation; given a mass distribution, compute its gravitational field; given a function, compute its integral. An inverse problem runs the other way: instead of giving a cause and observing its effect(s), we observe an effect and ask what caused it.
